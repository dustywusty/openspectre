After clearing it, Next level carries score and lives forward and refills hull and ammunition. Encounter mode instead ends when you destroy the red enemies. Type-7 tanks pursue you and shoot using firing rates and ranges from the original game data; scenery provides cover. Clear their escorts to expose protected type-31 targets. Tank 1 starts with six hull points and 40 rounds. Campaign advancement can upgrade shields, ammunition capacity, and speed. Flag missions start with three lives; surviving death restores hull and ammo while keeping collected flags and enemies as they are. Flags and enemy kills use recovered awards and the original default-difficulty score table. Player respawns use recovered search and clearance rules, with provisional random generation and collision fallback where required. Enemies consume their original life budgets and can borrow a life from another enemy of the same type before retiring. Surviving enemies respawn; Kills and Retired are tracked separately. Permanently retiring the last enemy awards the recovered enemy-clear bonus. Clearing the arena shows the recovered Quickness, Accuracy, and Avoidance ratings and awards their bonuses. Original transition movies and timing remain unfinished. R restarts the round. Tab or keypad * cycles views while the canvas is focused; +/− zooms the exterior and overhead views. Encounter AI, hull damage, and objectives are provisional rules. Stationary research mode preserves the previous behavior. The original disc supplies models, static placements, enemy types, base health, and sound samples. Player handling, projectile motion, and the type-31 vulnerability transition use recovered routines. Ice preserves momentum, static Cybermud slows you down, and Flow Fields push the tank where recovered contact geometry is available. Blue pickups grant flight energy: slowing down raises the tank, and accelerating lowers it. The tank descends when energy runs out. Down transit devices take you below ground and clear flight energy; up transit devices return you to the surface. Flight energy and altitude appear below the arena. Surface status appears there too. Initial spawn positions, campaign progression, camera, colors, and parts of collision remain provisional.
